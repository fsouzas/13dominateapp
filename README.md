## Features

- Import `standings.csv` and `heroes.csv` to generate nice-looking standings.
- Choose from custom themes.
- Save the standings as a PNG on your device.
- Share the generated standings.

## Installation

Just download the APK in the Releases section and install.

## How to Use

1. Export `standings.csv` and `heroes.csv` from GEM.
2. Open 13 Dominate Standings.
3. Select the two CSV files.
4. Choose your event format and store.
5. Generate your standings.
6. Save or share the resulting standings.
